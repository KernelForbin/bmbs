What this site can do

bmbs.bet turns your group's daily home run picks into a live scoreboard, tracking every leg against real MLB games as they happen — so you always know exactly where every bet stands.

Live tracking, no refreshing

  • Every leg updates on its own about every 20 seconds, straight from MLB's real game data — leave the tab open and watch it change.
  • The instant a home run shows up in the play-by-play, that leg turns green — even while the game is still going.
  • If a player gets pulled for a pinch hitter, that leg is marked missed right away instead of making you wait for the game to end.
  • A player whose team never takes the field for them is marked "Didn't play" and doesn't count as a loss.

Live at-bat context

  • While a game's in progress, see the current inning and whether your guy is up now or coming up soon.
  • AT THE PLATE NOW and GUARANTEED TO BAT THIS INNING flag legs about to be decided.
  • An estimated at-bats-remaining count and rough odds of another trip to the plate, based on league-average outcomes.
  • One tap jumps straight to the live play-by-play on MLB Gameday.

Today's & Yesterday's Picks

  • The current slate stays live and tracked all the way through midnight — a 10pm game doesn't get cut off by the calendar flipping over.
  • Once the very last game from that slate finishes, it moves automatically to Yesterday's Picks with final results.
  • Today's Picks then waits for the next slate, and shows a queued heads-up if tomorrow's picks are already in but tonight isn't over yet.

Running payout estimate

  • See an estimated total payout at the top of the page, updating live as bets hit.
  • If a leg is voided for a no-show player, the payout recalculates automatically for the legs still in play.

Tap-to-filter scoreboard

  • Quick counts for Batting Now / Batting Soon, and for every bet and leg status — Open, Hit, Missed, N/A, Live, Not Started.
  • Tap any number to filter the whole page down to just that group. Tap it again to clear the filter.

Bettor Tracker

  • A collapsible leaderboard showing each person's hits, misses, and their average + best hit odds for the slate.
  • Tap a name to filter the whole page to just that person's legs.

Parlays & single bets

  • Multi-leg parlay cards show every leg's status at a glance, and gray out automatically the moment any one leg misses.
  • Straight single-player bets get their own tracker alongside the parlays.
